1. The board has sixteen numbered spots

The current TAKEOVER page uses a fixed 4×4 grid with sixteen numbered spots. Each spot has a current price and one current owner or is available. An available spot begins at the displayed starting price. Prices and ownership shown in the browser may change while another customer is purchasing.

2. Pay the valid takeover price and the spot becomes yours

To take an occupied spot, you must complete a valid payment at or above the current required takeover level. The minimum increase is displayed in the purchase flow and may be changed globally by TAKEOVER.

When a valid takeover completes, the previous owner's placement ends for that spot. The previous owner is not ordinarily refunded merely because another advertiser paid more.

3. Multiple spots must connect

A multi-spot purchase must form one connected territory: every selected cell must connect to the selection through an edge-touching chain. Diagonal contact alone does not count.

If you already own cells included in a larger purchase, those cells remain yours and do not create a second charge merely for being included. Including them can merge the selected cells into a new shared creative territory.

4. Each territory can be designed in SPOT CANVAS

Territory owners may use TAKEOVER's structured editor to choose backgrounds, gradients, uploaded images, text, buttons, fonts, colors, positioning, size, motion, and Combined or Divided display. The purchased territory is the design surface.

TAKEOVER does not provide arbitrary HTML, JavaScript, CSS injection, or executable widgets to advertisers. Creative content is stored as structured settings plus approved uploaded image files. This preserves advertiser flexibility without granting control over the surrounding website.

Separate territories owned by the same advertiser may use different creative designs. Buying additional connected cells while including already-owned cells can create one larger shared creative.

5. Combined creatives keep their original footprint

When a Combined multi-spot creative is created, TAKEOVER records the territory footprint used to design it. If another advertiser later takes one or more cells, the original creative does not shrink, re-center, or reflow to fill the remaining shape.

Instead, the advertiser's remaining owned cells act as windows into the original canvas. Lost cells become holes—similar to dead pixels in the larger composition—while the artwork keeps its original scale and position. If enough cells are taken to split the territory into disconnected pieces, each remaining piece still shows the portion of the original creative corresponding to those cells.

Divided display intentionally behaves differently: each owned cell can continue showing its repeated single-cell design independently.

6. Owners may defend a spot

A current owner may raise the level of a spot they own. When TAKEOVER offers this defense control, the owner pays the disclosed difference between the existing level and the new level. A defense does not create permanent ownership; another advertiser can later pay the next valid takeover price, or the placement can otherwise end under the Terms.

7. Owning all sixteen creates a full-page takeover

A successful purchase resulting in ownership of all sixteen spots activates the full-page presentation and a 24-hour protection period. During that period, competing takeovers are blocked unless TAKEOVER must intervene for moderation, security, payment, legal, or operational reasons.

After the protection period expires, the owner continues to hold the page until a competitor validly takes at least one spot or another applicable end condition in the Terms occurs. If a competitor later takes a spot, the full-page Combined creative remains anchored to its original sixteen-cell footprint and the lost spot becomes a hole in that composition rather than causing the remaining artwork to resize.

8. The server resolves simultaneous purchases

Opening Stripe Checkout does not reserve a spot. TAKEOVER revalidates ownership, current prices, and full-page protection when a paid transaction completes. If two customers pursue the same territory, the first transaction that validly completes against the current board wins.

If a payment completes after the quoted territory has become stale, TAKEOVER is designed to reject the stale assignment and automatically refund the affected payment rather than overwrite a valid newer owner. See the Payments Policy for details.

9. Content rules are deliberately relaxed

TAKEOVER is not intended to police taste. Weird, ugly, provocative, competitive, sarcastic, annoying, unconventional, or unpopular advertising is generally allowed. Territory owners remain solely responsible for the legality and accuracy of what they publish and for any dealings with visitors.

The operator may manually reset a spot only for the narrow emergency categories described in the Advertising Content Policy: explicit nudity, pornography, or graphic sexual content; fraud or scams; malware, phishing, or malicious redirects; illegal or clearly dangerous activity; impersonation or serious rights abuse; credible threats or material safety concerns; or another comparably serious legal or platform risk.

A reset returns the affected spot to available. Moderation actions may be privately logged with a reason. A reset does not automatically create a refund right, and the existence of this power does not mean TAKEOVER pre-screens or continuously monitors every ad.

Report a serious problem: Use the Contact page with the spot number, advertiser name, and why you believe it crosses one of the narrow red lines above.

10. The board is not guaranteed to operate forever

TAKEOVER may pause purchases, temporarily take the page offline, migrate infrastructure, modify the service, or permanently discontinue all or part of the service for maintenance, security, legal, provider, technical, financial, business, or other operational reasons.

A spot purchase does not create a right to force TAKEOVER, its operator, the domain, or the board to remain online. Temporary downtime does not automatically extend a placement or a 24-hour protection period. If the service permanently ends, remaining placement rights end with it. Refund treatment is governed by the Payments Policy and applicable law.
