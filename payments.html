Payments & refunds.

TAKEOVER uses Stripe to process card payments for spot takeovers, multi-spot purchases, full-page takeovers, and defenses.

1. When you are charged

Before Stripe Checkout opens, TAKEOVER validates your selected spots and the levels you entered. Checkout shows the total amount to be charged. By completing Checkout after accepting the purchase terms, you authorize that charge for the advertising placement described.

TAKEOVER does not intentionally store full card numbers or card security codes in its application database. Stripe handles the payment interface and payment credentials.

2. Being outbid is not a refund event

A takeover is a completed advertising purchase, not a refundable deposit. If you successfully take a spot and another advertiser later pays more to take that spot from you, your earlier payment is ordinarily final. The time you controlled the spot may be short or long; TAKEOVER does not guarantee a minimum duration.

3. Stale Checkout protection

Another advertiser may change the board while you are completing Stripe Checkout. After Stripe reports a successful payment, TAKEOVER checks the affected spots again using a locked server-side transaction.

If the paid levels can no longer claim the entire quoted territory, TAKEOVER does not partially assign the purchase. The attempt is marked stale and TAKEOVER attempts to refund that payment automatically through Stripe.

Card-network timing can cause a refund to appear as a reversal or as a separate refund after the original charge. Your bank controls final posting times.

4. Defending your own spot

When you raise the level of a spot you already own, Checkout charges only the displayed difference between the existing level and the new level. That payment raises the spot's takeover requirement but does not guarantee any minimum additional display time.

5. Outages, suspension, and permanent shutdown

Temporary downtime does not automatically create a refund. Maintenance, hosting failures, DNS or domain issues, payment or authentication outages, security incidents, network problems, third-party provider failures, or other interruptions may temporarily make the page or a feature unavailable. Purchases do not include a guaranteed uptime percentage, impression count, traffic level, or minimum display duration.

If TAKEOVER permanently discontinues the service, remaining placement rights end when the applicable service ends. A shutdown does not create liability for lost exposure, anticipated traffic, leads, sales, profits, publicity, or other consequential losses.

If TAKEOVER determines that a specific completed transaction was not meaningfully fulfilled because of TAKEOVER's own technical or operational error, or if a refund is required by law, TAKEOVER may refund that specific transaction. To the fullest extent permitted by law, any monetary remedy relating to that transaction will not exceed the amount paid directly to TAKEOVER for that transaction.

6. Other refunds

Refunds may also be issued for duplicate charges, material technical errors, transactions TAKEOVER cannot fulfill because of its own error, customer-service resolutions, goodwill, fraud concerns, or other circumstances where a refund is required by law or approved by TAKEOVER. TAKEOVER may issue a full or partial refund through Stripe.

A refund and a moderation reset are separate actions. Unless TAKEOVER expressly resets the associated territory, refunding some or all of a payment does not by itself transfer or remove the advertiser's currently owned cells. When a refund is paired with a territory reset, only cells still tied to that exact purchase are eligible to be returned to available.

Advertisements removed because of fraud, malicious content, impersonation, infringement, prohibited goods or services, or another intentional policy violation may be ineligible for refund to the extent permitted by law.

TAKEOVER does not promise refunds simply because an advertisement receives fewer clicks, visits, impressions, leads, or sales than expected.

7. Taxes and processing

Any applicable taxes or other required charges may be collected where required. Payment availability, card acceptance, refunds, and dispute handling are also subject to Stripe and card-network rules.

8. Payment questions and disputes

If a payment appears incorrect, contact TAKEOVER support with the email used for the purchase, approximate time, company name, and affected spot numbers. Do not send full card numbers or passwords.

Contacting support does not remove any rights you may have through your card issuer or applicable law, but giving TAKEOVER an opportunity to review the server and payment records can often resolve an issue faster.
