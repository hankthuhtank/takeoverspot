Privacy Policy.

This policy explains the information TAKEOVER uses to run passwordless accounts, spot ownership, payments, public advertisements, support, security, and service operations.

1. Information we collect

TAKEOVER may process your email address, authentication identifiers, display name, brand name or alias, optional destination link, logo or image uploads, the spots you control, takeover and defense amounts, payment status and payment-provider identifiers, support requests, timestamps, and technical records needed to prevent abuse and diagnose errors.

When passwordless sign-in is used, authentication is handled through Supabase. TAKEOVER does not need to create or store a site-specific password for that flow.

2. Information displayed publicly

TAKEOVER is a public creative-placement page. Display names, aliases, brand names, logos, optional destination links, creative designs, occupied spot locations, and current displayed spot prices are public. Visitors should assume those materials can be viewed, linked, screenshotted, cached, indexed, or shared.

Email addresses, private authentication identifiers, Stripe payment identifiers, full purchase records, and losing takeover attempts are not intended to be displayed publicly.

3. How information is used

  • authenticate territory owners and let them manage owned spots;
  • display and update public creative placements;
  • validate takeovers and defenses;
  • process payments and automatic stale-checkout refunds;
  • send outbid, payment, security, and account messages;
  • moderate content and investigate fraud or abuse;
  • provide support and comply with legal obligations.

4. Service providers

TAKEOVER uses infrastructure providers to operate the service. These may include Supabase for authentication, database, storage and server functions; Stripe for payment processing; GitHub Pages for website hosting; and an email-delivery provider such as Resend if transactional email is enabled.

Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. Card details entered into Stripe-hosted payment interfaces are handled by Stripe rather than intentionally stored as full card numbers in TAKEOVER's application database.

5. Retention

Current spot ownership and public creative data are retained while needed to operate the live page. Purchase, payment, refund, moderation, support, fraud-prevention, and audit records may be retained for business, accounting, security, dispute, and legal purposes.

Uploaded creative assets may remain in backups or storage for a reasonable period after a spot changes hands. TAKEOVER may automatically delete old unreferenced creative assets under its storage-retention process.

6. Your choices and requests

You can update the display name or alias, optional destination link, and logo associated with spots you own through your TAKEOVER account. You may contact support to request access, correction, deletion, or other privacy rights where applicable. Some transaction, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, and audit records may need to be retained despite a deletion request.

7. Security

TAKEOVER uses authentication, row-level database controls, restricted storage paths, server-side payment validation, locked spot updates, and third-party payment processing to reduce risk. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

If you believe your account, payment, or displayed identity has been misused, contact support promptly.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as TAKEOVER changes providers or features. Material changes apply prospectively and the effective date above will be revised.
