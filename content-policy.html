1. Use only what you are authorized to use

You must own or have permission to use the company name, trade name, logo, trademark, image, and website you submit. Do not impersonate a business, person, public agency, or organization you are not authorized to represent.

2. Prohibited advertising

TAKEOVER may reject or remove advertising involving illegal goods or services, fraud, scams, phishing, malware, deceptive redirects, impersonation, stolen or counterfeit goods, intellectual-property infringement, explicit sexual content, extremist or terrorist promotion, unlawful discrimination, credible threats, or other content that creates material legal, security, or safety risk.

TAKEOVER may also refuse advertisements for regulated categories when the operator cannot reasonably support the compliance obligations associated with them.

3. Ad format

The standard ad is intentionally limited: company identity, an optional logo/image, and a destination website. TAKEOVER does not accept advertiser-supplied HTML, JavaScript, embedded forms, browser scripts, executable files, or arbitrary widgets.

Logo uploads are limited by file type and size. TAKEOVER may resize or compress visual assets for display.

4. Destination websites

Your linked destination must be a legitimate HTTP or HTTPS website and must not intentionally distribute malware, steal credentials, trigger abusive downloads, mislead visitors about the advertiser's identity, or redirect to prohibited content.

You are responsible for claims and representations made on your destination site. TAKEOVER does not endorse or verify every external website.

5. Review, removal, and repeat abuse

TAKEOVER may remove an ad immediately when there is a reasonable security, fraud, infringement, legal, or safety concern. The operator may request evidence that you control a brand or destination.

Accounts associated with repeated violations, charge fraud, impersonation, malicious links, or attempts to bypass moderation may be restricted. Refund eligibility after removal is governed by the Payments Policy and applicable law.

Report an ad: Use the Contact page and include the spot number, advertiser name, and reason for your report.
