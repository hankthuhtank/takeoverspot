LEGAL / AD POLICY

Keep it open. Keep it sane.

TAKEOVER is intentionally permissive. Territory owners get wide creative freedom, while the operator keeps a narrow emergency reset right for genuinely harmful, fraudulent, illegal, malicious, or high-risk placements.

1. TAKEOVER is not the taste police

Placements are not removed merely because they are weird, loud, ugly, provocative, competitive, sarcastic, annoying, unconventional, artistic, noncommercial, or disliked by another person. TAKEOVER is designed to let owners have personality and take creative risks.

The operator generally intervenes only when a placement crosses a meaningful fraud, security, legal, safety, impersonation, or rights-abuse line, or presents another similarly extreme risk to users or the platform.

2. The territory owner is responsible for the content

TAKEOVER provides the placement. The owner provides the identity, artwork, message and optional link. The owner is solely responsible for the legality, safety, accuracy and rights to everything submitted or linked from a placement.

A placement can be commercial, artistic, personal, promotional or pseudonymous. Displaying it does not mean TAKEOVER has investigated, verified, sponsored, recommended, or endorsed its owner or message.

3. Use only what you are authorized to use

You may publicly use a real name, brand name, creator name, project name, handle, or pseudonym. You must own or have permission to use every name, logo, trademark, photograph, graphic, artwork, creative asset, and linked destination you submit. Do not use a pseudonym to impersonate a business, person, public agency, creator, or organization you are not authorized to represent.

4. The short red-line list

TAKEOVER may reset or remove a placement when there is a reasonable basis to believe it involves one of the following extreme categories:

Explicit nudity, pornography, or graphic sexual content; fraud or scams; malware, phishing, credential theft, or malicious redirects; illegal or clearly dangerous activity; impersonation or serious unauthorized use of another party's rights; credible threats or material safety concerns; or another comparably serious legal or platform risk.

This list is deliberately short. The operator retains final discretion for unusual edge cases, but ordinary disagreement, offense, poor taste, bad design, or aggressive marketing alone is not intended to trigger moderation.

5. Claims and pricing belong to the advertiser

Advertisers should not knowingly publish materially false or deceptive statements about price, savings, inventory, earnings, performance, credentials, guarantees, sponsorship, affiliation, product origin, or what a customer will receive. An offer or pricing line should describe a real offer available from that advertiser under the conditions it represents.

The advertiser—not TAKEOVER—is responsible for advertising and consumer-protection rules that apply to the advertiser's industry and location.

6. Territory format

Every owned territory can be designed with SPOT CANVAS. Owners may use safe structured controls for backgrounds, colors, gradients, uploaded images, logos, text, buttons, fonts, sizing, positioning, destination links, and Combined or Divided display. Separate territories may use separate designs.

Combined creatives keep their original purchased footprint. If cells are lost, those cells become holes in the composition while the remaining owned cells continue to show the corresponding portion of the original artwork. TAKEOVER clips content so it never appears inside territory the owner no longer controls.

TAKEOVER does not accept advertiser-supplied HTML, JavaScript, executable files, embedded forms, browser scripts, or arbitrary widgets. Uploaded images are restricted by file type and size and may be resized or cropped for presentation.

7. Optional destination links

A link is optional. If you leave it blank, the territory is display-only and does not open another page. If you provide one, it must be a legitimate HTTP or HTTPS destination and must not intentionally distribute malware, steal credentials, trigger abusive downloads, impersonate another party, or redirect visitors into content covered by the extreme red-line list.

You are solely responsible for content, claims, transactions, customer data practices, fulfillment, refunds, warranties, and conduct on your destination site. Visitors should independently evaluate an advertiser before transacting.

8. Emergency moderation and takedown

TAKEOVER does not undertake a duty to investigate or pre-screen every advertisement before it appears. A placement may therefore appear before a complaint or risk is identified.

When an extreme issue is credibly reported or discovered, TAKEOVER may reset the affected spot to available immediately, restrict an account, preserve relevant records, or request evidence that the advertiser controls a brand, image, claim, license, or destination. A moderation reset does not automatically create a refund right.

Owner moderation actions may be privately logged with a reason and note for accountability. The existence of this reset power does not create an obligation to monitor every placement continuously.

Report a serious problem: Use the Contact page and include the spot number, advertiser name, destination link if one exists, and why you believe it crosses one of the red lines above.
