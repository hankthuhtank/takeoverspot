1. The advertiser is responsible for the ad

TAKEOVER provides the placement. The advertiser provides the message. The advertiser is solely responsible for the truthfulness, legality, substantiation, safety, and accuracy of everything submitted or linked from an ad, including descriptions, prices, discounts, offers, qualifications, availability, testimonials, warranties, product claims, and service claims.

Displaying an advertisement does not mean TAKEOVER has investigated, verified, licensed, certified, sponsored, recommended, or endorsed the advertiser. TAKEOVER is not a party to transactions between advertisers and visitors.

2. Use only what you are authorized to use

You must own or have permission to use every company name, trade name, logo, trademark, photograph, graphic, testimonial, creative asset, and website you submit. Do not impersonate a business, person, public agency, or organization you are not authorized to represent.

3. Prohibited advertising

TAKEOVER may reject or remove advertising involving illegal goods or services, fraud, scams, phishing, malware, deceptive redirects, impersonation, stolen or counterfeit goods, intellectual-property infringement, explicit sexual content, extremist or terrorist promotion, unlawful discrimination, credible threats, or other content that creates material legal, security, or safety risk.

TAKEOVER may also refuse advertisements for regulated categories when the operator cannot reasonably support the compliance obligations associated with them.

4. No fraudulent, deceptive, or unsupported claims

Do not publish materially false or misleading statements about price, savings, inventory, earnings, performance, credentials, licensing, guarantees, health or safety, sponsorship, affiliation, product origin, or what a customer will receive. An offer or pricing line must accurately describe a real offer available from the advertiser under the disclosed conditions.

You are responsible for complying with advertising and consumer-protection rules that apply to your industry and location. TAKEOVER does not provide compliance approval merely by allowing a placement to render.

5. Ad format

A single spot uses the compact presentation: company identity, optional logo, and destination website. When an advertiser controls two or more touching spots and selects Combined, TAKEOVER may also display a short description, one offer or pricing line, and one feature image. Advertisers may instead select Divided to preserve individual cell boundaries.

Combined creative automatically adapts to the territory actually owned. If cells are lost, TAKEOVER may resize, rearrange, crop, simplify, hide rich fields, or revert the remaining cell to the compact presentation. This is an expected part of the product, not an alteration requiring advertiser approval.

TAKEOVER does not accept advertiser-supplied HTML, JavaScript, executable files, embedded forms, browser scripts, or arbitrary widgets. Uploaded images are restricted by file type and size and may be resized or cropped for presentation.

6. Destination websites

Your linked destination must be a legitimate HTTP or HTTPS website and must not intentionally distribute malware, steal credentials, trigger abusive downloads, mislead visitors about the advertiser's identity, or redirect to prohibited content.

You are solely responsible for content, claims, transactions, customer data practices, fulfillment, refunds, warranties, and conduct on your destination site. Visitors should independently evaluate an advertiser before transacting.

7. No duty to pre-screen; review and takedown

TAKEOVER may use automated or manual review but does not undertake a duty to investigate or pre-screen every advertisement before it appears. A placement can therefore appear before a complaint or risk is identified.

TAKEOVER may remove or disable an ad immediately when there is a reasonable fraud, scam, security, infringement, impersonation, legal, safety, or policy concern. The operator may request evidence that an advertiser controls a brand, image, claim, license, or destination.

Accounts associated with repeated violations, payment fraud, impersonation, malicious links, or attempts to bypass moderation may be restricted. Refund eligibility after removal is governed by the Payments Policy, Terms, and applicable law.

Report a suspicious ad: Use the Contact page and include the spot number, advertiser name, destination website, and reason for your report. TAKEOVER may temporarily disable reported material while it is reviewed.
