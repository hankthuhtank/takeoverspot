1. Reporting copyright or other rights concerns

Use the TAKEOVER Contact page and choose Copyright / trademark / rights report. Identify the protected work or right, the TAKEOVER spot or advertiser material you believe infringes it, where the original or authorized work can be found, and enough information for TAKEOVER to understand and investigate the claim.

For a copyright notice intended to satisfy applicable U.S. notice-and-takedown requirements, include: identification of the copyrighted work; identification and location of the allegedly infringing material; your contact information; a statement of good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or law; a statement that the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act for the copyright owner; and your physical or electronic signature.

2. Review and takedown

TAKEOVER may promptly disable or remove material when a credible rights complaint is received, preserve relevant records, notify the advertiser, request additional information, and restrict repeat abuse. Removal may occur before the parties resolve the underlying dispute.

TAKEOVER does not promise to adjudicate ownership disputes. The platform may keep disputed material offline while the parties resolve the matter or while a legally sufficient counter-notice process runs.

3. Counter-notices

An advertiser who believes material was removed because of mistake or misidentification may submit a counter-notice through the Contact page. The request should identify the removed material, explain the basis for the claimed mistake, provide the legally required statements and contact information, and include a physical or electronic signature where applicable.

Where a statutory counter-notice procedure applies, TAKEOVER may forward the counter-notice to the complaining party and restore material only when legally appropriate.

4. Repeat infringement

TAKEOVER may restrict or terminate access for advertisers who repeatedly infringe copyright, trademark, publicity, privacy, or other third-party rights when the circumstances reasonably support that conclusion. The platform may consider the number, credibility, and disposition of prior complaints rather than applying an automatic one-strike rule.

5. False or abusive reports

Do not knowingly submit materially false infringement notices or counter-notices. A person making a rights complaint is responsible for the accuracy and legal basis of the complaint. TAKEOVER may preserve reports and related records where necessary for disputes, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Submit a report: Use REPORT / CONTACT and select Copyright / trademark / rights report.
