How TAKEOVER protects transactions

01

Stripe Checkout

Card-entry and payment processing are handled by Stripe rather than collecting full card numbers in the TAKEOVER page.

02

Passwordless access

Advertisers can use secure email sign-in links instead of creating another site-specific password.

03

Server-side ownership

Spot prices and ownership are validated and changed on the backend rather than trusted to browser-side code.

Before opening a card dispute

If a transaction looks wrong, contact support first so TAKEOVER can review the server-side spot state, takeover attempt, Stripe payment status, and any automatic refund attempt. This does not take away rights you have through your bank, card network, or applicable law.
